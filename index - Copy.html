Biography

The Hawds are an independent band from Stroud, Gloucestershire, UK, playing a mix of rock, funk, country, and jazz. You can listen to their tracks on Spotify or view their profile on Visit Gloucestershire.

Based out of DB Studios in Stroud, UK (and frequently at the Woolpack pub in Slad).

Band Details:
• Style: Rock, funk, country, and jazz mix
• Members: Andrew Butler, Nigel Prenter, Mark Butler, Dave Butler, Mick Duff, Andy Gilliams, Mike Candler, and Dave Miles
