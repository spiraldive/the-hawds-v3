Flawlessly fused rock, funk, country and jazz.

Biography

The Hawds are a 6-piece band from Stroud in Gloucestershire who performed at WOMAD in 2024. Individually they have performed on the BBC to an audience of 3.2 million and at Wembley Stadium, recorded with Mick Jagger and Trevor Horn, and played in the Yardbirds and Iggy Pop's band. Their material is self-penned, eclectic and layered with harmonies. Their latest, of nine, albums is titled Light in Times of Darkness.

Based out of DB Studios in Stroud, UK (and frequently at the Woolpack pub in Slad).
